Our Story

From Farm to Your Doorstep

FreshMart was founded in 2018 with a simple mission: make fresh, organic groceries accessible to everyone. What started as a small local delivery service in Chennai has grown into a platform serving over 50,000 families across Tamil Nadu.

We partner directly with 200+ certified organic farmers, cutting out the middlemen so you get the freshest produce at fair prices — and farmers get a better livelihood.
